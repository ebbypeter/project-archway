== User Journeys

=== Planning an inspection round (Asset Planner)

. The Asset Planner reviews due and overdue inspections in the Field
  Inspection App's planning module, grouped by route.
. They assign a week's route to a field crew; the app pre-downloads asset
  records, forms and defect catalogues for offline use.
. Progress is visible as crews sync; slipped work is re-planned the same day.

=== Inspecting in the field (Field Crew)

. At the asset, the crew opens the pre-downloaded inspection form — no
  connectivity required.
. They capture condition ratings, defect codes and photos; entries are
  validated on-device against the form rules and GPS-stamped.
. Completed inspections queue locally (encrypted at rest, per the offline-first
  ADR).
. When coverage returns, the app uploads the queue to the Asset Sync Service
  with resumable, at-least-once delivery.

=== From upload to AssetHub (automated)

. The Asset Sync Service validates each payload: schema, defect catalogue
  versions, plausibility rules (e.g. condition score deltas).
. Valid results update asset condition records in AssetHub the same day;
  rejects go to a review queue with the reason attached.
. Conflicts (same asset edited twice) resolve last-writer-wins per field, with
  both versions kept in the audit trail.
