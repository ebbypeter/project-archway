== Capabilities

The solution realises five capabilities within the *Asset Management* business
capability. The table maps each to the containers that provide it.

[cols="2,3,2",options="header"]
|===
| Capability | What it provides | Realised by
| Inspection Planning | Schedule and assign inspections by route, asset class and due date; planners see coverage and overdue work. | Field Inspection App (planning module), AssetHub (work orders)
| Offline Field Data Capture | Structured inspection forms, photos and defect codes captured with no connectivity; GPS-stamped and queued locally. | Field Inspection App
| Validation & Data Quality | Payload validation against asset schemas, defect-code catalogues and plausibility rules before anything reaches AssetHub. | Asset Sync Service
| Asset Condition Management | Validated results update condition scores and trigger follow-up work recommendations. | Asset Sync Service, AssetHub
| Synchronisation & Conflict Resolution | At-least-once upload with resumable transfer; last-writer-wins per field with audit trail when the same asset is edited twice. | Field Inspection App, Asset Sync Service
|===

=== Capability boundaries

Deliberately *out of scope* for this solution:

* Asset investment planning and valuation — stays in AssetHub.
* Analytics and condition trend reporting — InsightLake consumes AssetHub data
  via the existing `AssetHub-InsightLake` integration; this solution adds no
  direct analytics feed.
* Workforce identity — delegated to IdentityCloud (OIDC).
