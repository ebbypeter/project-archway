== InsightLake

InsightLake is the enterprise analytics platform. It receives data from operational
and asset systems, curates it, and serves reports and dashboards to the business.

=== Responsibilities

* Ingesting operational telemetry and asset data
* Curated data products and semantic models
* Reports and dashboards for analysts and business managers
